ITALY ESIM FAQ

Questions before you connect

Check plan choice, setup, calls, hotspot use and regional coverage before purchasing.More destinations

07 practical answers
Choose a plan

Best option and data

01What is the best eSIM for Italy?+

The best Italy eSIM depends on your trip length, data use, budget and required features. Compare coverage, allowance, validity, hotspot rules and support—not only price.

02How much data do I need?+

Light navigation and messaging may use 1–3 GB per week. Regular social media, video calls or hotspot use can require 5–10 GB or an unlimited plan.

Set up your eSIM

Install and activate

03When should I install and activate my Italy eSIM?+

Install the eSIM over Wi-Fi before departure, but check when validity begins. Some plans activate after connecting locally; others begin at installation or purchase.

04Can I keep using my regular number?+

Yes. Use the travel eSIM for data while keeping your primary SIM available for calls or messages, subject to your home carrier’s roaming charges.

Use it abroad

Calls, hotspot and borders

05Do Italy eSIM plans include calls and SMS?+

Many travel eSIMs are data-only. Calls and messages can still work through apps such as WhatsApp, FaceTime or Signal. Choose a plan with a phone number only when traditional calls or SMS are required.

06Can I share Italy eSIM data through a hotspot?+

Hotspot sharing depends on the provider and product. Fixed-data plans often permit tethering, while unlimited plans may apply a daily sharing limit. Verify the plan terms before purchase.

07Will one Italy eSIM work in nearby countries?+

A Italy-only plan may stop working after you cross the border. If your itinerary includes multiple countries, choose a regional eSIM and verify that every destination appears in its coverage list.
